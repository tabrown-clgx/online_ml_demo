How it works

Each photo was encoded once by a frozen SigLIP2 image encoder; the two class descriptions were encoded by its text encoder. The starting "zero-shot" classifier simply compares a photo with the two descriptions. Every label you give retrains a small linear classifier on top of the frozen embeddings, starting from that text-based guess and pulled back toward it, with both classes weighted equally. Nothing leaves your browser and nothing is saved.

Held-out photos are never used for training. They were chosen to include photos the zero-shot classifier finds hard, so its starting score is lower than it would be on typical photos. With 20 held-out photos per task, each one is worth 5 points. Improvement is not guaranteed.
